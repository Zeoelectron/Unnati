Capacitance with partial dielectric
Two regions side-by-side → parallel combination

\( C_1 = \dfrac{k\varepsilon_0 (A/2)}{d} = \dfrac{k\varepsilon_0 A}{2d} \) (dielectric \(k\))
\( C_2 = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 (A/2)}{d} = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{2d} \) (air)

\( C_{\text{eq}} = C_1 + C_2 = \dfrac{(k+1)\varepsilon_0 A}{2d} \)
